## 4.1 Audit of AI-Generated CAPM Assumptions

To establish a rigorous financial benchmark for estimating the present value of Zheng Qinwen’s future commercial earnings, the baseline report generated by ChatGPT (GPT-5.5 Thinking) applied an unverified, flat discount rate of **12.0%**. The absence of granular, disclosed inputs severely compromised the model's reliability.

Through iterative financial auditing prompts, the implied Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM) framework behind the 12.0% baseline was reverse-engineered as follows:

* **Risk-Free Rate ($R_f$)**: $4.0\%$, based on a generic long-term U.S. Treasury yield.
* **Beta Coefficient ($\beta$)**: $1.60$, selected under the premise that professional athlete earnings exhibit higher volatility than the broader equity market.
* **Equity Risk Premium (ERP)**: $5.0\%$, mirroring mature market baseline expectations.

$$\text{Reconstructed Implied CAPM: } r = R_f + \beta \times \text{ERP} = 4.0\% + 1.6 \times 5.0\% = 12.0\%$$

---

## 4.2 Conceptual Weaknesses of the AI Approach

### 4.2.1 Geographic Misalignment and Currency Mismatch
Standard valuation theory dictates that the risk-free rate must synchronize strictly with the currency denomination and jurisdiction of the underlying cash flows. 
* While Zheng Qinwen competes on the global WTA circuit (receiving USD prize money), her primary brand equity is concentrated in the Chinese consumer market through major endorsement contracts with domestic and localized luxury brands (Audi China, Dior, Vivo). 
* Applying a single static U.S. Treasury rate ignores cross-border inflation differentials and purchasing power dynamics.

### 4.2.2 Corporate Diversification vs. Concentrated Human Capital Risk
* Traditional CAPM assumes investors are only compensated for systematic market risk, as unsystematic risk can be eliminated via portfolio diversification (*Leifheit & Follert, 2021*).
* In stark contrast, Zheng operates as a **"Pure Play" individual asset**: her commercial cash flows are 100% correlated with her singular physical presence, performance consistency, and public appeal (*Kahn, 2000*). 
* For an individual athlete, idiosyncratic risks (injuries, form loss) effectively transform into **non-diversifiable systematic exposure**. Hence, a corporate benchmark Beta of 1.6 substantially underestimates downside volatility.

## 4.3 Quantitative Derivation of the Independent Model

Our independent analysis replaces the arbitrary AI baseline with an empirically grounded **Modified CAPM + CRP + ARP** framework.

### 4.3.1 Revenue-Weighted Risk-Free Rate ($R_{f,\text{final}}$)
To accurately mirror multi-jurisdictional revenue streams, we structure a dual-currency weighted formula:

$$R_{f,\text{final}} = \left(w_{\text{Global}} \times R_{f,\text{US}}\right) + \left(w_{\text{Domestic}} \times \left[R_{f,\text{China}} + \text{Spread}_{\text{Inflation}}\right]\right)$$

* $w_{\text{Global}} = 40.0\%$ (2025 on-field prize money of \$1.6M + global sponsorships \$7.44M / \$22.6M total).
* $R_{f,\text{US}} = 4.0\%$ (10-Year U.S. Treasury yield baseline).
* $w_{\text{Domestic}} = 60.0\%$ (China-centric commercial contracts of \$13.56M / \$22.6M total).
* $R_{f,\text{China}} = 1.75\%$ (2026 China 10-Year Government Bond yield).
* $\text{Spread}_{\text{Inflation}} = 2.92\%$ (Macroeconomic spread preserving real purchasing power valuation).

$$R_{f,\text{final}} = (0.40 \times 4.0\%) + (0.60 \times [1.75\% + 2.92\%]) = 1.60\% + 2.802\% = 4.402\% \approx \mathbf{4.40\%}$$

### 4.3.2 Implied Equity Risk Premium (ERP) Adjustment
Following empirical data from *Damodaran (2025)*, mature equity markets reflect an elevated implied ERP ($\text{ERP}_{\text{Mature}}$) of 5.50%. We further incorporate a sports sector marketing sensitivity parameter ($\lambda_{\text{Sports}} = 0.30\%$):

$$\text{ERP}_{\text{final}} = \text{ERP}_{\text{Mature}} + \lambda_{\text{Sports}} = 5.50\% + 0.30\% = \mathbf{5.80\%}$$

### 4.3.3 Pure-Play Asset Beta Estimation ($\beta_{\text{final}}$)
We unbundle corporate diversification by applying an asset specificity multiplier ($\alpha_{\text{Specificity}}$):

$$\beta_{\text{final}} = \beta_{\text{Unlevered}} \times \alpha_{\text{Specificity}} = 1.30 \times 1.423 = \mathbf{1.85}$$

* $\beta_{\text{Unlevered}} = 1.30$: Observable asset Beta for global Media, Sports & Entertainment.
* $\alpha_{\text{Specificity}} = 1.423$: Operational leverage adjustment accounting for zero intra-firm diversification.

### 4.3.4 Country (CRP) and Athlete-Specific (ARP) Risk Premiums
* **Country Risk Premium ($\text{CRP} = \mathbf{0.80\%}$)**: Derived from Damodaran’s adjusted sovereign default spreads for emerging luxury consumption markets.
* **Athlete Risk Premium ($\text{ARP} = \mathbf{2.50\%}$)**:
  * *Physical Injury Factor ($1.50\%$)*: Grounded in WTA load-capacity models, accounting for power-baseline strain and past elbow injuries triggering reduction clauses.
  * *Career Longevity & Reputation Factor ($1.00\%$)*: Capturing compressed female athletic peak earning windows and personal brand vulnerability.

---

## 4.4 Consolidated Cost of Equity Formulation

Integrating all derived parameters into the final discount rate ($r$):

$$r = R_f + (\beta \times \text{ERP}) + \text{CRP} + \text{ARP}$$

$$r = 4.40\% + (1.85 \times 5.80\%) + 0.80\% + 2.50\%$$

$$r = 4.40\% + 10.73\% + 0.80\% + 2.50\% = 18.43\% \longrightarrow \mathbf{18.40\%}$$
